PAST SIMPLE (V2 / -ed) Completed past actions & specific dates

Used for completed actions in chronological order or events tied to a specific past time (in 1202, in 1611, when, after).

"In 1202, Fibonacci published Liber Abaci."

PAST PERFECT (had + V3) The "past before the past"

Used for an earlier action that happened before another past event or deadline (signal words: before, by the time, until, already, even though).

"Before he wrote the book, he had traveled across North Africa."
